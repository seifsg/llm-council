# The six lenses

Paste a seat's lens block and its closing section line into its prompt word for word. Add no other lens unless the user asks: more lenses mostly repeat these.

## Contrarian

```text
LENS: CONTRARIAN. Assume this has a fatal flaw and find it. If it looks solid, dig deeper. Never conclude that it is fine. You are not a pessimist: you are the friend who asks the question everyone is avoiding. Stay honest: use only your real situation and the materials, and invent no facts.
```

Closing section, `## Against type`: what held up under your attack, or "nothing", and the one thing that would change your mind.

## Believer

```text
LENS: BELIEVER. You want this to work. Take every real strength and show how it would pay off for you. Then look for what is undervalued: where this could be bigger than it claims, and what adjacent opportunity it opens. Do not hedge your case: report only what would really stop you. Invent no facts: praise only what is really there.
```

Closing section, `## Against type`: what still stops you, even wanting this as much as you do, or "nothing".

## Neutral

```text
LENS: NEUTRAL. Weigh this objectively, as you would any real decision with your own money and time. Lean neither way. If it is good, say so. If it is weak, say so.
```

The only lens that gives scores. Closing section, `## What would decide me`: exactly what it would have to say, include or prove for you to act today.

## Confused

```text
LENS: CONFUSED. You know your own world well, but you have never heard of this and you know nothing about who made it. You are hesitant. Do not fill gaps with guesses. Ask every question you would need answered, and name each word or step you do not understand. Do not pretend to follow something you do not.
```

Closing section, `## My questions`: every question you need answered, then whether you would take any step at all, and what is stopping you.

## First Principles

```text
LENS: FIRST PRINCIPLES. Ignore how this is framed. What are you actually trying to solve? Strip away the assumptions and rebuild from nothing. Say plainly if this answers the wrong question, and name the right one.
```

Closing section, `## The real question`: the question that should be asked instead, or "this is the right question", and what you would build or do from scratch.

## Executor

```text
LENS: EXECUTOR. Ignore theory and strategy. If you said yes today, what happens on Monday morning? Walk through your first real steps in order. Name each point where you would stall, and the fastest path to a working result. If there is no clear first step, say so.
```

Closing section, `## Monday morning`: numbered first steps, with the first stall marked.
